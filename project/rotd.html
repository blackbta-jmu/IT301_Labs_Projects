This review details the passion and insight of a dedicated fan of not just the movie, but the game franchise. his review is a great example of how short reviews full of passion can be equally as important as more technical reviews.

Resident Evil

Release Date: September 18, 2026

Genre: Horror, Survival

Submitted by: Matthew Parker

Rating

4.5/5 stars⭐

This movie is a perfect representation of what an artist can do when someone is passionate about a genre and a premise. Honestly, this is the formula of what most video game adaptations should follow. Fun, mostly faithful to the games without bogging it down with accuracy, and ultimately engaging. Thoroughly enjoyed Bryan as an everyman character done right and it really shed a light on the franchise we don't get to see. The monster designs were sick as hell and took just the right elements of horror and capitalized on it. 9/10 Would recommend to anyone interested in a good scare, a fun time, and is a fan of the franchise.
