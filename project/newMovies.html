Clayface

Release Date: October 23, 2026

Genre: Horror, Thriller, Superhero

A rising Hollywood actor undergoes a terrifying trnasformation after a medical treatment changes his appearance and shakes his grip on reality. This dark DC story explores identity, revenge, and the danger of scientific ambition and vanity.

Why watch it? A darker take on a classic Batman villain with strong themes of body horror.

Resident Evil

Release Date: September 18, 2026

Genre: Horror, Survival

A medical courier finds himself caught in a terrifying outbreak as one night turns into a fight for survival. This new entry reboots the Resident Evil franchise with a blend of horror, comedy, and action. Fans of the video game series will find familiar elements, while newcomers will be drawn into a suspenseful and thrilling story.

Why watch it? A new take on the Resident Evil made for fans of the video game series.

Wildwood

Release Date: October 23, 2026

Genre: Animation, Adventure, Fantasy

When a young girl's baby brother is taken by a murder of crows, she ventures into a mysterious wilderness filled with strange creatures and dangerous secrets. She must find the courage to rescure him and protect a magical world.

Why watch it? A beautifully crafted adventure, with stop motion animation from the animators of Coraline.
